# Saree Design AI - IP & Data Policy

This project strictly adheres to the following guidelines before any data enters the training pipeline:

## 1. Ownership & Rights
Ownership of any AI-generated textile design depends heavily on the source rights of the input images, the terms of service of the foundational AI model used (e.g. SDXL), and local jurisdiction laws regarding AI copyright. This pipeline does NOT guarantee outright legal ownership. Creators must ensure they possess commercial rights to all training inputs to claim any downstream rights.

## 2. Artist Consent & Attribution
- Do **not** scrape, download, or train on specific artist or brand-owned saree designs without explicit, documented permission.
- All dataset images must either be owned by the user, explicitly licensed for commercial generative usage, or public domain.

## 3. Trademark Rules
Ensure no trademarked logos, watermarks, or protected brand-specific motifs are present in the training data to prevent accidental memorization.

## 4. Data Retention & Rejection
- **Rejection**: Any image in the dataset review phase lacking clear provenance or violating artist rights must be rejected.
- **Retention**: Raw dataset images and references must be stored securely. Tokens/Passwords must never be committed to version control.
